# ImageGen Prompt: front master v2

## Input roles

- Original character image: source of truth for facial identity, eye shape, sleepy expression, painterly softness, hair color, and signature costume motifs.
- Front master v1: reference only for the new front-facing neutral rigging composition and clean isolated silhouette.

## Improvement target

Generate a new canonical front master from scratch.

Retain the rig-friendly frontal composition of v1, but correct the following:

- move facial identity and rendering closer to the original character
- preserve the original's mature, elegant, gently sleepy impression
- avoid a generic cute-girl face
- make the eye shape slightly narrower and calmer
- keep lavender eyes with detailed reflections
- reduce excessive costume ornaments and tiny bows
- simplify hair into controllable large locks
- keep all hair and clothing away from the canvas edges
- frame from the top of the hair to the upper hip

## Prompt

Create a completely new illustration. Do not edit, crop, trace, or warp either input.

The character must be unmistakably the same original lavender-haired moon-and-stars woman shown in the original reference, while using a new perfectly front-facing rigging pose.

Composition:

- eye-level, perfectly front-facing
- head, shoulders, and torso have no tilt or twist
- centered from top of hair to upper hip
- both arms slightly away from the torso
- relaxed hands visible near the lower corners
- generous padding around hair, sleeves, and hands
- nothing touches or crosses the canvas boundary
- no props or scenery

Design:

- long lavender-periwinkle hair, organized into back hair, two side locks, and front bangs
- calm violet eyes, slightly narrow and softly sleepy
- elegant young adult proportions rather than childlike proportions
- signature crescent moon, dangling stars, and one lavender bow on character-left/viewer-right
- star earrings and lavender moon pendant choker
- lavender lace nightdress
- off-white oversized knit cardigan with restrained moon and star embroidery
- remove unnecessary repeated bows and dense decorations

Rendering:

- match the original reference's soft, refined anime illustration and subtle painterly shading
- clean enough for later separated-part regeneration
- stable facial symmetry and clean silhouette
- opaque hair edges, no flyaway translucent wisps
- neutral closed-mouth smile and open eyes
- no text, watermark, particles, bloom, or depth of field
- uniform solid chroma green RGB #00FF00 with no gradient, shadow, texture, or green spill
- 3:4 portrait
