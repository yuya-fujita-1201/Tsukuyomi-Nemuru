# ImageGen Prompt: v4 left three-quarter pose

Use case: reference-based pose generation

Use the user-authored faceless front, left-facing full-body profile, and face
close-up as references. Generate the exact same character turned about 35°
toward viewer-left, with calm neutral eyes and a small closed mouth. Match the
front reference's 1086×1448 bust crop, head size, shoulder scale, vertical
placement, outfit, hair, ornaments, line weight, and pastel shading.

The background must be flat vivid green. Do not generate a full-body image,
scenery, stars, moon, floor, shadow, text, border, or extra figures.
