# ImageGen Prompt: view-right 3/4 pose

## Input roles

- Original reference: secondary identity and mood reference.
- `front-master-v2-chroma.png`: canonical source of truth for face, proportions, costume, line weight, colors, canvas, and design.

## Direction convention

`view-right` means the character's nose points toward the viewer's right side of the image.

## Prompt

Generate a new illustration of the canonical character in a controlled right-facing three-quarter pose.

- same 1086×1448-style 3:4 canvas and scale as the front master
- same head size and vertical placement
- head turns about 25 degrees toward viewer-right
- upper torso turns about 8 degrees toward viewer-right
- shoulders remain nearly level
- eyes look slightly toward viewer-right
- neutral closed-mouth smile
- both arms remain low and relaxed
- preserve the physical left/right placement of the moon ornament and bow; do not mirror the character design
- keep identical costume construction, colors, rendering, and ornament count
- keep all visible contours inside the canvas with generous margin
- use uniform chroma green `#00FF00`

Do not add props, scenery, text, shadows, glow, particles, or new accessories.
